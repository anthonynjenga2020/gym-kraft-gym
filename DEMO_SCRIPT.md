# 🎬 Demo Video Script — Stone Systems Gym Website Product
**Format:** Loom · ~2.5 minutes · PiP Webcam (bottom-right corner)
**Screen:** Live gym website (Kraft Gym demo) + Review Funnel page

---

## LOOM SETUP (Before You Record)

### Step 1 — Download & Install Loom
1. Go to **loom.com** and sign up (free tier works fine for demos)
2. Download the **Loom Desktop App** — it gives you the best PiP control
3. Install it and sign in

### Step 2 — Set Up the PiP Webcam Bubble
1. Open Loom → click **New Recording**
2. Choose **"Screen + Cam"** (not just Screen)
3. Under camera options, you'll see a **bubble preview** of your face in the bottom-right — that's your PiP
4. You can resize the bubble (small / medium / large) — **medium** looks best for product demos
5. Move it to the **bottom-right corner** so it doesn't cover your UI
6. Set recording to **Full Screen** or **Window** (choose your browser window with the website open)

### Step 3 — Pre-Recording Checklist
- [ ] Open the Kraft Gym demo site in Chrome, **zoom to 90%** so the full page is visible
- [ ] Also have the **Review Funnel page** (`/review`) open in a separate tab
- [ ] Close all notification popups and Slack/email
- [ ] Use a clean, solid background behind you (or blur background in Loom settings)
- [ ] Mic check — use a headset or external mic if available, avoid laptop mic
- [ ] Do one 10-second test recording to check framing

## TIPS FOR A GREAT DEMO VIDEO

- **Keep cursor movement deliberate** — don't dart around randomly. Move to what you're talking about.
- **Pause before switching tabs** — give viewers a beat to register the transition
- **Record 2-3 takes** — pick the best one. Takes under 3 min are easy to redo
- **PiP size:** Medium bubble, bottom-right. Big enough to feel personal, small enough not to block UI
- **Lighting:** Face a window or use a ring light — soft, even light looks professional on camera
- **Loom free plan** = up to 25 videos, unlimited views. More than enough for demos.
